# Reviews and Social Media Audit: One Source Medical Center / "One Medical Clinic" (1972 W 5400 S, Suite #2, Taylorsville, UT 84129)

> Method and confidence: research done 2026-10-20 with WebSearch only. WebFetch failed with `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND` (tested on onesourcemvc.com), so no page could be opened in full. Most findings come from search-engine snippets or the search tool's summaries of them. Verbatim quotes are marked with quotation marks; everything else is paraphrase. "Not found in search index" means it was not found, not that it does not exist. Do not credit any "One Source Medical" pages from Texas, Florida, Arizona, New Jersey or Nevada, or Amazon One Medical (onemedical.com); they are listed below as look-alikes.

## 1. Patient reviews on every platform (ratings, counts, dates, verbatim text, themes, owner replies, signs of fake reviews)

### Takeaway
No reviews were found on any platform for the "One Source Medical Center" / "One Medical Clinic" brand itself: no Google rating, Yelp, Facebook, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, etc. The patient reviews tied to this address are filed under a co-located sister brand, **AVA Injury Clinic** (an auto-accident chiropractic clinic). Its reviews are very positive but thin in visible text: Birdeye shows 4.9★ from 223 reviews, Wheree 4.9★ from 24, and Yelp 5.0★ from 1 review (October 2025). Much of the text is in Spanish and centres on warm, friendly staff. No negative reviews and no owner replies were visible in the index.

### Inferences
- **The One Source brand has no reputation of its own online.** Anyone who searches "One Medical Clinic Taylorsville" finds no stars, and the name clashes with Amazon's One Medical: a search for "One Medical Clinic Taylorsville google reviews" returned only Amazon One Medical Yelp pages in Seattle, Washington DC, New York and Tysons ([Yelp example](https://www.yelp.com/biz/one-medical-downtown-seattle-seattle)). For an agency this is the main finding: the reputation at this address sits under AVA Injury Clinic, not One Source.
- **Possible signs of incentivised or low-quality reviews (none proven):**
  - Review counts swing widely across aggregators (1, 24, 203 and 223).
  - Ratings are a near-uniform 4.9 with no visible negative review.
  - The aggregator reports perks: drinks, Uber rides, and "free" appointments.
  - Reviews are reposted as marketing content.
  - A directory miscategorises the clinic as a law firm.
  - These patterns are common in lien-based auto-injury clinics. They show the reviews need checking; they do not prove the reviews are fake. The full Google review list (dates, reviewer history, bursts) would have to be pulled to judge.
- **HIPAA:** no owner replies were visible, so no reply-based exposure of health information could be assessed. Reposting patients' public reviews (Threads) is lower risk, but staff should never confirm treatment details in replies.
- **Compliance flag (inference, for counsel to judge):** the NPI record for the same-address entity (One Source Care LLC) says it accepts Molina Healthcare (Medicaid managed care). Free rides or "free appointments" offered to Medicaid patients can draw beneficiary-inducement scrutiny.
- **Date of the Threads post:** decoding Instagram/Threads shortcode `DUmSaOfkepm` (media ID >> 23 + Instagram epoch) gives about **2026-02-11 UTC**. This is derived, not displayed.
