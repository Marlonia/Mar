# Business gaps: One Source Medical Center / One Medical Clinic (Taylorsville, UT) and sister brand AVA Injury Clinic

Research date: 2026-10-20. Method: about 32 WebSearch queries in standard and extended mode. Per the assignment, WebFetch and direct HTTP were not used because they fail in this environment, so every finding comes from search-engine snippets and the summaries the search tool wrote of them. Those snippets can paraphrase. Status labels: **CONFIRMED** means a named source's snippet states it directly. **INFERRED** means it is my reasoning from cited facts. **NOT FOUND** means searches turned up nothing. "Age" notes such as "~391 days old" come from the search tool's metadata on the indexed page.

### Cited Findings
- **CONFIRMED:** ONE MEDICAL, Serial 87095933, **Reg. 5219859**, owner 1Life Healthcare, Inc. (Delaware corporation, P.O. Box 26170, San Francisco, CA 94126). Status "700 – Registered". Filed 2016-07-07, registered 2017-06-06, first use 2016-09-01. Primary class **044**: medical care services including primary care, family medicine and management of chronic conditions, plus health counseling and wellness services and an internet-based prescription renewal service — [Justia Trademarks](https://trademarks.justia.com/870/95/one-87095933.html); [uspto.report](https://uspto.report/TM/87095933)

- **CONFIRMED:** The 5219859 Section 8 deadline was 2023-06-06, with a grace period to 2023-12-06. Snippets did not confirm whether the filing was made — [uspto.report](https://uspto.report/TM/87095933)

- **CONFIRMED:** Reg. **3874262** appears as a "Prior Registration" on the 5219859 record. Its mark and services were not shown — [Justia Trademarks](https://trademarks.justia.com/870/95/one-87095933.html)

Registration 3808181 is incontestable. The design-only nature of 5219859 limits it somewhat, but likelihood-of-confusion analysis looks at the word portion, and 3808181 supports rights in the words.
